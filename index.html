    About Dispatch

    A live globe and map of geolocated conflict, climate, and political events, refreshed daily. Nothing here is simulated — every point traces back to a real tracked event or report.

    Conflict

    GDELT's global event database, filtered to protest, coercion, and violence events — real individual incidents, refreshed every 15 minutes.

    Climate

    NASA EONET — real tracked wildfires, storms, floods, drought, and temperature extremes.

    Political

    GDELT's global event database, filtered to diplomacy, aid, sanctions, and cooperation events.

    Shipping lanes & chokepoints

    Digitized trade-route geometry and the world's 10 busiest maritime chokepoints, compiled from public shipping and energy-transit data — a snapshot, not live vessel tracking.
